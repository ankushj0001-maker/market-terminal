Index prices come from Yahoo Finance's public chart endpoint and headlines from each publisher's official RSS feed (Economic Times, Moneycontrol, LiveMint, Business Standard, rbi.org.in) — only the original headline, source and timestamp are shown; read the full story on the publisher's own site. Data is collected by a scheduled job roughly every 15 minutes and published as a static file, rather than fetched live by your browser, so a single visitor's network or browser settings can't block it.

For information only — not investment advice. Figures reflect the last scheduled update, not tick-by-tick pricing; check the status dot on each card and the "data as of" time before relying on a number. Use the refresh button to check for a newer scheduled update at any time.
